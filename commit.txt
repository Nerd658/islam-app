[2026-08-02] — Fix: Add vocalized Arabic letter names (nameAr) for clear 100% TTS letter audio

What changed:
- **arabic_learning.json**: Added `nameAr` (vocalized Arabic name like "تَاء", "بَاء", "ثَاء", "جِيم") for all 28 letters of the alphabet.
- **ArabicAlphabet.jsx**: Updated letter click and Listen button handlers to send `letter.nameAr` to `playAudio`.

Why:
- Fixes issue where isolated unvocalized consonants like bare "ت" or "ث" were silent in TTS engines because unvocalized consonants cannot be spoken without vowels or full names.

- FRONTEND/islam app/src/pages/arabic/ArabicAlphabet.jsx
