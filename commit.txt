2026-08-07 — Design System Enforcement & UX Enhancements

What changed:
- **Zakat**: Set default currency to FCFA (XOF). Replaced hardcoded black/gray colors with Design System tokens (`theme-surface`, `theme-border`, `theme-accent`).
- **Qibla**: Applied Design System tokens. The compass needle now highlights in Gold (`theme-accent`) when precisely aligned with Mecca.

Why:
