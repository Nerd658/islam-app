[2026-08-01] — Feature: Add Qibla Compass and Chatbot Memory

What changed:
- Chatbot Memory: Updated `Chat.jsx` to maintain and send the entire conversational history array to the backend. Updated `chatController.js` to parse this history array instead of just a single message, allowing the OpenRouter AI to remember the context of the conversation.
- Qibla Compass: Created a new `/qibla` page allowing users to find the exact direction of the Kaaba from their current geolocation.
- Utilized HTML5 Geolocation API to fetch user coordinates and DeviceOrientation events to detect phone rotation and smoothly align the on-screen compass needle.

Why:
- The virtual Imam couldn't hold a continuous conversation without context.
- A Qibla compass is an essential tool for an Islamic application, making it easier for users to pray while traveling or in unfamiliar locations.
