[2026-08-01] — UI/UX: Add search and pagination to 99 Names page

What changed:
- Added a search bar allowing users to filter names by Arabic text, transliteration (e.g. "Rahman"), or English meaning.
- Added pagination to the 99 Names page, displaying 20 names per page.
- Created beautiful pagination controls with `ChevronLeft` and `ChevronRight` from `lucide-react`.

Why:
- Scrolling through 99 names on a single page can be overwhelming and bad for performance.
- Users requested the ability to quickly look up a specific name or its meaning without scrolling indefinitely.

Files affected:
- FRONTEND/islam app/src/pages/Names.jsx

Breaking changes:
- None.
