[2026-08-01] — Feature: Quran Audio & 99 Names of Allah (v2.2.0)

What changed:
- **Quran Audio**: Implemented full audio recitation support in `QuranReader.jsx`. Users can now listen to the Quran verse by verse (recited by Mishari Rashid al-`Afasy). Features include Play/Pause on individual verses, auto-playing the next verse, and real-time UI highlighting of the currently playing verse.
- **99 Names of Allah**: Created a beautiful `Names.jsx` gallery that fetches and displays the Asma ul-Husna (Arabic, transliteration, and meaning) using a modern glassmorphism grid.
- **Home UI**: Added a gorgeous banner on the `Home.jsx` page to access the 99 Names of Allah without cluttering the bottom navigation bar.

Why:
- Added upon user request (`/goal 1 et 3`) to bring the app closer to a premium, full-featured Islamic companion app. The auto-advancing audio recitation and the 99 Names gallery significantly enrich the spiritual value and user retention of the app.

Files affected:
- FRONTEND/islam app/src/components/QuranReader.jsx — Integrated audio API and player states.
- FRONTEND/islam app/src/pages/Names.jsx (New) — 99 Names feature.
- FRONTEND/islam app/src/pages/Home.jsx — Added banner link.
- FRONTEND/islam app/src/App.jsx — Added route.
