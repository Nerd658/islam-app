2026-08-07 — Apply Premium Design System to Quran Page & Global Layout

What changed:
- Removed hardcoded `bg-black` and `text-gray-100` from `App.jsx` layout, replacing them with global CSS variables `bg-theme-bg` and `text-theme-text`.
- Refactored `QuranReader.jsx` Surah list cards: added `bg-theme-surface-hover`, inner gradients, emerald box-shadow on hover, and smooth scaling effects.
- Replaced hardcoded `text-white` in Quran verses with `text-theme-text` for softer contrast.
- Replaced `bg-[#0a0a0a]` and `bg-[#111]` with `bg-theme-surface` across the reader.
- Audio playback UI: Currently playing verse gets a green inner gradient `from-theme-primary/10`, and the active recited word highlights in emerald.
- Replaced the hardcoded 'text-white' Arabic text rendering in the reader with softer `text-theme-text` and emerald borders for verse numbers.

Why:
- The app layout was forcing a pure black background that prevented pages from inheriting the softer `theme-bg` variable.
- The Quran page had legacy hardcoded colors (`#111`, `#222`, `text-white`) which felt flat and lacked the premium depth created in recent components like Tasbih and Adhkar.
- Contrast issues on pure black backgrounds made the pure white text aggressive to read.

Files affected:
- FRONTEND/islam app/src/App.jsx — Global background layout tokens updated.
- FRONTEND/islam app/src/components/QuranReader.jsx — Complete UI token overhaul for cards, verses, and controls.

Breaking changes:
- None.

Notes for review:
