[2026-08-02] — Fix: Remove crossOrigin property from HTML5 Audio to resolve Google TTS CORS block

What changed:
- Removed `audio.crossOrigin = 'anonymous'` in `src/utils/audio.js`.

Why:
- Setting `crossOrigin = 'anonymous'` on Google TTS endpoints caused browsers to reject media loading due to CORS pre-flight restrictions on Google servers. Removing `crossOrigin` allows standard un-credentialed media streaming.

Files affected:
- FRONTEND/islam app/src/utils/audio.js

Breaking changes:
- None.
