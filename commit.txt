[2026-08-01] — Feature: Geolocation, Hijri Date, and Adhkar

What changed:
- Added a "Locate Me" (📍 Me localiser automatiquement) button in `App.jsx` using `navigator.geolocation` and Nominatim reverse geocoding to instantly find the user's city and fetch prayer times.
- Updated `PrayerTimesList.jsx` to dynamically extract and prominently display the Islamic Hijri Date.
- Highlighted the critical fasting times (Fajr and Maghrib) in the UI for better UX during Ramadan and fasting days.
- Added a new `AdhkarList.jsx` component that provides morning and evening invocations (Adhkar) with Arabic text, French translation, and repetition counts.
- Created `adhkar.json` to store invocation data.

Why:
- Entering city/country manually adds friction; geolocation provides instant access.
- The Hijri calendar is essential for Muslims to track Islamic months and fasting days.
- Adhkar are a daily necessity for a practicing Muslim. Providing them in the app increases its daily utility and spiritual value.

Files affected:
- BACKEND/server.js — Included Hijri date in the API response.
- FRONTEND/islam app/src/App.jsx — Added Geolocation logic, Nominatim API reverse geocoding, and imported AdhkarList.
- FRONTEND/islam app/src/components/PrayerTimesList.jsx — Refactored to extract and highlight Hijri Date and Fajr/Maghrib.
- FRONTEND/islam app/src/components/AdhkarList.jsx — New component for reading Invocations.
- FRONTEND/islam app/src/data/adhkar.json — Data source for the invocations.

Breaking changes:
- None.
