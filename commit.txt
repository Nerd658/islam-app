[2026-08-02] — Fix: Synchronize audio timeupdate wordIdx directly with rendered Tajweed tokens

What changed:
- Updated `handleTimeUpdate` in `QuranReader.jsx` to compute `currentWordIndex` directly from the tokenized Tajweed HTML string tokens.

Why:
- Previously `handleTimeUpdate` looked for `activeVerseObj.words` which could be undefined or mismatched with rendered Tajweed tokens, locking `currentWordIndex` at 0. Calculating from rendered tokens makes the green underline advance fluidly from word 0 to 1 to 2 to 3 across every verse.

Files affected:
- FRONTEND/islam app/src/components/QuranReader.jsx

Breaking changes:
- None.
